Quick Transcribe Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Quick Transcribe is published by Zan Cimperman (“the publisher”, “we”). For privacy questions or requests, contact cimperman.work@gmail.com.

This policy covers the Quick Transcribe iPhone app and its Messages extension and Shortcuts actions. It explains what the app processes on your device, when data leaves it, and what the publisher receives.

Choose where transcription happens

  • On-device: WhisperKit processes recordings on your iPhone after the selected model has downloaded. Quick Transcribe does not upload your audio or transcript for this transcription. Model downloads contact Hugging Face and its delivery infrastructure for the Argmax model repository; these services receive connection information, including your IP address and the requested model files, rather than your recording.
  • Groq: using your own Groq API key, the app sends the recording, selected language where specified, model choice, and request parameters directly to Groq over HTTPS. Groq also receives the API key for authentication and connection information such as your IP address. Groq returns the transcript and associated response information. The publisher does not receive a copy through a publisher-operated transcription server.
  • Automatic: the app uses Groq only when you have saved a Groq key and turned on “Allow sending audio to Groq”; otherwise it uses On-device. If Groq is rate-limited, unreachable, times out or is temporarily unavailable and an on-device model is downloaded, that recording is transcribed on the device instead. Select On-device if you never want audio to leave your iPhone.

The app asks for your explicit permission before any audio is sent to Groq. You can withdraw it at any time in Settings → Cloud (Groq); no recording is uploaded while it is off.

Groq is a separate service used through your own account. Its applicable agreements, privacy policy, and data controls documentation govern its processing. Groq states that inference inputs and outputs are not retained by default, but may be logged for reliability or abuse investigations for up to 30 days, or longer when legally required. Usage metadata is retained separately. Organization administrators can enable Zero Data Retention controls in Groq; Quick Transcribe does not enable or verify those controls. Groq states that retained customer data is stored in the United States and that standard contractual clauses are available where applicable. Check your account’s terms and settings before sending recordings, particularly sensitive information.

Copying, sharing, Messages, and Shortcuts

Copying a transcript uses a device-local pasteboard entry with a five-minute expiry. Automatic copying can be disabled in Settings. Apps into which you paste text receive their own copy.

Messages offers Preview first, Insert automatically, and Send automatically. Preview first lets you edit before inserting; Insert automatically puts text in the conversation’s compose field; Send automatically attempts to send the transcript immediately after transcription without another review step. Messages, Apple’s messaging infrastructure, and the conversation recipients handle the text under their own settings and policies. Deleting a transcript in Quick Transcribe does not recall a message.

The Share action and Shortcuts pass data to the apps, actions, or recipients you select. An audio-file Shortcut’s original input remains under the control of its source app; deleting Quick Transcribe’s temporary copy does not delete that original. Review the destination’s privacy practices before sharing.

Information received by the publisher

The current app has no publisher user account, publisher-hosted transcript history, advertising SDK, or analytics SDK. We do not sell app recordings or transcripts or use them for advertising. Normal transcription does not send recordings, transcripts, or API keys to the publisher.

If you contact cimperman.work@gmail.com, we receive your email address, your message, and any attachments or diagnostics you choose to send. We use them to answer your request, investigate the reported problem, and maintain relevant support or privacy-request records. The publisher uses Gmail, so Google and the email providers delivering the correspondence also process it. Google describes its handling of email and international processing in its privacy policy. Google operates servers globally; correspondence may be processed outside the EU/EEA, including in the United States, under its applicable transfer safeguards. Provide only the information needed to resolve your request; an API key or private recording is not required.

We keep correspondence only as long as needed to resolve and follow up on the request, meet applicable legal obligations, or establish or defend legal claims. The relevant criteria are the request’s nature, whether follow-up remains necessary, and any applicable legal retention requirement. You can request deletion at the privacy contact above. We do not promise that deletion from our correspondence deletes copies controlled by your email provider or other recipients.

Legal bases and your rights

Where EU/EEA data protection law applies to processing for which the publisher is responsible, providing the app functions you request relies on performance of our contract with you. Handling voluntary support correspondence relies on our legitimate interests in answering requests and resolving problems; handling statutory privacy requests and legally required records relies on compliance with legal obligations. A microphone permission is a device access choice, not blanket consent to every use of a recording. Groq’s processing through your account has its own applicable legal bases and agreements. If a recording contains sensitive personal data or another person’s speech, make sure you have the necessary legal basis and permissions before recording or uploading it.

Subject to the conditions in applicable law, you may request access, correction, erasure, restriction, or portability of personal data we hold, and object to processing based on legitimate interests. Where processing relies on consent, you can withdraw it without affecting earlier lawful processing. Contact cimperman.work@gmail.com to exercise your rights. We may request information reasonably needed to verify the request and will respond within the applicable legal period. We cannot remotely access or erase files held only on your device; use the app controls above. For data held by Groq through your account, use Groq’s privacy contact or account controls.

You can complain to the supervisory authority where you live or work or where an alleged infringement occurred. Information about these rights is available from the European Commission. Quick Transcribe does not make decisions with legal or similarly significant effects about you through automated profiling.

Children and policy changes

Quick Transcribe is not directed to children. Third-party services have their own eligibility rules; using the app does not override them.

We may update this policy when our practices change. The date above identifies this version. Material changes will be described with the relevant app update or policy notice.
